--- layout: alternative tool: hoppscotch title: A Hoppscotch alternative for gRPC, SignalR and AMQP — Bowire description: Bowire vs. Hoppscotch — when each fits, where they differ feature by feature, and how to move over today via OpenAPI or discovery. Local-first, Apache 2.0. lede: Hoppscotch is a fast, open-source client that runs in the browser. Bowire adds gRPC, SignalR, MCP and the messaging protocols, plus record & replay, mocks and security scanning. ---

Bowire has no importer for Hoppscotch's collection JSON. What works today:

  • Point Bowire at the service. Discovery reads the OpenAPI document, the GraphQL schema (introspection) or gRPC reflection — no collection needed.
  • OpenAPI on disk. If your Hoppscotch collection came from an OpenAPI document, drop that document on Bowire's Schema Files tab.
  • Realtime. WebSocket, SSE, Socket.IO and MQTT connect by URL in Bowire as they do in Hoppscotch; see protocols.
