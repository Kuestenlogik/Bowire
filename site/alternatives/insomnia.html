Bowire discovers your services instead of keeping a hand-maintained collection, speaks more protocols, and needs no account. ---

Bowire has no Insomnia importer yet — that is #674. What works today:

  • Point Bowire at the service. For OpenAPI, gRPC (reflection) and GraphQL services, discovery lists every method without any import.
  • OpenAPI. Export the spec with Insomnia's Inso CLI and drop the file on Bowire's Schema Files tab.
  • HAR. Insomnia exports HAR; bowire import har export.har turns it into a .bwr recording you can replay, mock or turn into tests (--redact-secrets strips credential headers). See file formats.
