Local-first, Apache 2.0. lede: Bruno keeps collections as plain files in git. Bowire shares the local-first stance and adds discovery, record & replay, mocks and many more protocols. ---

Bowire has no .bru importer yet — that is #674. Bruno exports a collection in other formats, and Bowire reads two of them today:

  • Postman Collection. Export the collection as a Postman Collection in Bruno, then click Import Postman in Bowire's Compose rail. Method, URL, headers and body carry over; scripts and tests don't. See Postman Collection import.
  • OpenAPI. Export as an OpenAPI specification and drop the file on Bowire's Schema Files tab.
  • Or skip the export and point Bowire at the running service — discovery covers OpenAPI, gRPC and GraphQL.
